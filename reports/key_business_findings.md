# Key Business Findings

1. **Scale:** 65,752 distinct orders across 5 markets and 23 regions generated $36,784,735 sales and $3,966,903 profit.
2. **Delivery:** 57.3% of order-item records exceeded their scheduled shipping duration. Average actual-vs-scheduled difference was 0.57 days.
3. **Shipping mode:** First Class has the highest observed SLA-breach rate (100.0%), while Standard Class has the lowest (39.8%). This is descriptive, not causal.
4. **Regional hotspot:** Central Africa has the highest observed regional SLA-breach rate at 60.7%.
5. **Profitability:** Strength Training has the lowest category-level profit margin among categories in the cleaned data at 0.61%.
6. **Profit leakage:** 33,784 order-item records (18.7%) have negative profit.
